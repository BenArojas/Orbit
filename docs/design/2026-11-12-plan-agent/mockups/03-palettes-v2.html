Palettes, round two: quieter, 60 / 30 / 10

Every accent here is desaturated and pulled toward warm or dusty tones, the way luxury and banking palettes work: a deep anchor, a soft light neutral, and one restrained metal or pastel used sparingly. Each palette is shown on the same mini app (nav, risk wallet, plan card, fire button) so you can compare them directly. Profit and loss colours are softened to match and appear only on money.
