Colour — the 60 / 30 / 10 question

Four triads, each shown as the same live plan card so they're comparable. The split is 60% base surface, 30% second colour (the one that carries identity — ink mass or structure), 10% loud (the fire button, armed state, live price). One hard constraint: profit green and loss red are data colours, not brand colours — they only ever appear on money and charts, tuned per palette so they sit on the base without fighting the loud accent.
