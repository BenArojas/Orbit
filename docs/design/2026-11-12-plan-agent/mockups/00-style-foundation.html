Orbit — style foundation, two directions

Not screens yet. This is the vocabulary the screens get built from: palette, type, edges, and the four shapes that carry the whole product — the money headline, the price rail, the condition list, and the fire button.

Numbers shown are illustrative (IREN, demo data). Pick a direction, or mix: "A's density with B's wording" is a valid answer.

Direction A — Terminal Darkevolution of today's Orbit
Inspiration

Bloomberg density, Linear restraint. Keeps Orbit's current dark identity but takes the neon away from decoration and spends it on meaning: green and red appear only on money, amber only on armed. Everything else is graphite. Dense, flat, reads fast at a glance mid-session.

Direction B — Daylight Calmfresh "simplified pro"
Inspiration

Apple Health meets a private-bank briefing. Paper surfaces, soft shadows, no hairlines, lots of air. Serif headlines so a plan reads like something written for you rather than a blotter dump. Colour is scarce: one indigo for "this is the action", muted green and clay for money. Deliberately not scary.
