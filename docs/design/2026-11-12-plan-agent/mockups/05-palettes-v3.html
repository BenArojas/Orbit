Palettes, round three: real 60 / 30 / 10 colour blocking

Round two felt flat because the 30% colour only appeared on thin lines, leaving every screen 95% dark grey. In those 60-30-10 design breakdowns, 60% and 30% are two distinct architectural zones (here: the 60% Workspace + the 30% Agent & Execution Dock), and 10% is a pure, punchy pigment (never muddy, never acid neon) that makes the whole combo pop.
